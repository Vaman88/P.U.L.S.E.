Good to know

A few practical details.

What files do I need?

A daily sales CSV and an inventory CSV for the same medications. The upload screen shows the required columns.

How are signals used?

Each medication’s model selects up to five relevant dated signals from the catalog using only its training history.

Can I update the plan?

Yes. Select newer sales and inventory files in your workspace to train a fresh local model and replace its encrypted plan.

Is this a clinical decision tool?

No. PULSE is a research demo. Forecasts and purchase guidance need review by qualified pharmacy staff.

What a signal means

Stable sig_ IDs identify origin, measure, cadence, geography, and entity. Check unit, definition, source, observation date, and horizon. Five-state demand value 4 is a highest relative category—not four units, prescriptions, or an order recommendation. Missing and unusable values remain explicit. Legacy FDA/NADAC IDs collapse incompatible measures; use the dimensioned IDs. Recall counts are dated FDA reports, not active local shortages.

News shadow model outputs are estimates, not observations, and stay unusable until compatible inputs pass chronological validation. Conflicting same-period values carry ambiguous=true.

Weekly surveillance signals

Find source observations with catalog searches such as fluview, nndss, and cdc_wval. These add disease context; they are not validated model inputs, prescriptions, or units to buy. Observation date, source timestamp, and ingest time mean different things.

History size & rate limits

Latest accepts 1,500 IDs; history accepts 100. More than 10,000 recorded source rows returns HTTP 413 without truncation. Retry with fewer IDs or a shorter range. HTTP 429 includes Retry-After seconds; public requests refill over time. Private browser training splits large batches automatically.

Coverage and freshness

Freshness reports catalog counts, latest non-shadow period, model target date, failed adapters, and news model status. Coverage separates usable values, recent source observations, and current-target projections. A current projection can use an older source. Recent means 14 days weekly, 60 monthly, 400 annual—display thresholds, not proof of upstream completeness.

Failed sources do not invalidate other verified data. Inspect row status and timestamps. Routes include /freshness, /coverage, /demand/drugs, /news/recent, and /sources/shortages/recent. News reports timestamp kind and sanitized source-check errors.

Source semantics

NADAC values are grouped by rate classification and pricing unit. Part D projections disclose method, accuracy, target year, and thresholds. Search is literal. Public routes are read-only; private files stay in the browser. Browse all operations in the interactive docs.
